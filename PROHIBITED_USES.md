# Prohibited Uses Policy

This file is incorporated by reference into the MotifAI Source License.
Use of the Software or any Derivative Work for the purposes below is prohibited.

Last Updated: 2026-03-24
Policy Version: 1.0

## 1. Prohibited Uses

The following uses are prohibited, whether direct, indirect, internal, external, paid, unpaid, pilot, or production.

1. Military operations, weapons development, weapons targeting, or battlefield decision support.
2. Defense or intelligence workflows intended to identify, track, profile, or engage people or groups.
3. Mass surveillance, including persistent population monitoring, large-scale behavioral tracking, or indiscriminate data fusion for monitoring civilians.
4. Suppression, oppression, or persecution of individuals or groups, including use to deny civil liberties, due process, or lawful dissent.
5. Any use that materially contributes to human rights abuses under internationally recognized human rights standards.

## 2. Clarifications

1. If a use case can reasonably fall within Section 1, it is prohibited.
2. Splitting functionality across services, subsidiaries, vendors, or contractors does not avoid this policy.
3. Repackaging the Software, exposing it through APIs, or embedding it in larger systems does not avoid this policy.

## 3. Reporting and Enforcement

1. Suspected violations may be reported to the project author with available evidence.
2. The author may update this policy to clarify scope and add examples.
